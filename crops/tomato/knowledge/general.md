# Tomato Agronomic Profile & Environmental Production Parameters

**Crop**: Tomato (*Solanum lycopersicum*)  
**Family**: Solanaceae  
**Topic**: General Agronomy & Growth Environment  

---

## 1. Temperature Thresholds & Phenology
- **Base Temperature (Tb)**: 10°C (growth ceases below this).
- **Chilling Injury**: Prolonged exposure to temperatures between 0°C and 10°C causes leaf chlorosis, delayed flowering, and fruit pitting.
- **Optimum Day Temperature**: 21°C to 27°C (70°F to 80°F).
- **Optimum Night Temperature**: 16°C to 19°C (61°F to 66°F).
- **Heat Stress Threshold**: Day temperatures >32°C (90°F) or night temperatures >21°C cause pollen sterility, style elongation, and catastrophic blossom drop.

## 2. Atmospheric Humidity (RH %)
- **Optimum Range**: 50% to 70%.
- **Low RH (<40%)**: High vapor pressure deficit (VPD) accelerates transpiration, triggering water stress even in moist soils; promotes two-spotted spider mite outbreaks.
- **High RH (>75%)**: Inhibits natural pollen shedding from anthers; provides required moisture films for fungal spore germination (*Alternaria*, *Botrytis*, *Passalora*).

## 3. Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium (NPK) Dynamics
- **Seedling & Vegetative**: Moderate Nitrogen, High Phosphorus (e.g., 10-20-10 or 1-2-1 ratio) to stimulate vigorous root branching.
- **Fruit Fill**: High Potassium (e.g., 1-1-2 or 1-1-3 ratio) to drive fruit sizing, lycopene synthesis, and osmotic regulation.
