# Healthy Tomato Plant Maintenance & Optimal Husbandry

**Crop**: Tomato (*Solanum lycopersicum*)  
**Status**: Healthy / Asymptomatic  
**Topic**: Preventive Husbandry & Growth Stage Management  

---

## 1. Characteristics of Healthy Tomato Plants
- **Foliage**: Uniform, deep green leaves with no chlorosis, necrotic spotting, curling, or water-soaked lesions.
- **Stem**: Sturdy, upright, fibrous main stem with active green apical meristem.
- **Root System**: White to light cream, fibrous root hairs without brown vascular discoloration or root galling.
- **Flowers & Fruit**: Vibrant yellow petals with strong pollen shedding; smooth, unblemished fruit development free of blossom end rot or catfacing.

## 2. Optimal Environmental Parameters
- **Soil Moisture**: 40% to 70% volumetric water content.
- **Soil pH**: 6.0 to 6.8 (ensures optimal macro- and micronutrient bio-availability).
- **Day Temperature**: 21°C to 27°C (70°F to 80°F).
- **Night Temperature**: 16°C to 18°C (61°F to 65°F).
- **Relative Humidity**: 50% to 70% (balances transpiration without promoting fungal sporulation).

## 3. Preventive Maintenance Regimen
1. **Weekly Scouting**: Inspect 10 plants per quadrant, checking both upper and lower leaf surfaces.
2. **Drip Irrigation Maintenance**: Check dripper emitters for clogging and test electrical conductivity (EC) of run-off.
3. **Pruning & Training**: Remove suckers (indeterminate types) weekly when they are under 5 cm long to channel carbohydrates to main fruit trusses.
4. **Preventive Nutrition**: Supply balanced N-P-K (1:1:2 ratio during fruit fill) plus soluble calcium (Ca(NO3)2) to prevent blossom end rot.
