# Tomato Target Spot — Disease Management Guide

**Crop**: Tomato (*Solanum lycopersicum*)  
**Disease**: Target Spot  
**Pathogen**: *Corynespora cassiicola* (Fungal)  
**Topic**: Plant Pathology & Foliar Diagnostics  

---

## 1. Etiology & Biology
*Corynespora cassiicola* is a necrotrophic fungus causing target spot on tomatoes, cucumbers, cotton, and soybeans. It survives on crop debris, volunteer plants, and alternate weed hosts across tropical and warm temperate zones.

## 2. Symptom Identification
- **Leaves**: Circular to irregular brown lesions with faint concentric zonation, often closely resembling early blight. Lesions lack the prominent yellow chlorotic halo typically seen around early blight spots.
- **Flowers & Stems**: Dark brown necrotic lesions on blossoms leading to blossom abortion and drop.
- **Fruit**: Small, pinpoint dark brown sunken specks that expand into large, crater-like brown lesions with split centers.

## 3. Favorable Environmental Conditions
- Temperatures of 20°C to 28°C (68°F to 82°F).
- High humidity (>80%) and prolonged leaf wetness (minimum 16–24 hours for severe infection).

## 4. Control Measures
1. **Air Circulation**: Stake and prune to open the lower canopy.
2. **Debris Destruction**: Thoroughly bury or remove crop residue immediately post-harvest.
3. **Fungicides**: Pyraclostrobin, Fluxapyroxad, Boscalid, Chlorothalonil, or Famoxadone + Cymoxanil. Ensure sprays penetrate deep into the lower interior canopy.
