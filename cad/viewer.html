DeepCrop Field Unit

The sensor node for one potted tomato plant. The ESP32-S3 sits in a sealed case on a stake. The soil probes go into the root zone, and the DHT22 hangs in a shield at canopy height.

The model is generated from cad/field_unit.py in the DeepCrop repository. The CAD files are in cad/out/: the full assembly as STEP for Fusion 360, FreeCAD or Onshape, and the printable case, lid and shield as STL. The plant shape is illustrative. Board sizes are nominal, so measure your own parts before printing.
